# Otaku-mori Home Portrait Late-Afternoon Vertical Slice Brief v1

**Status:** Owner-side production brief  
**Target asset:** `home.world.late-afternoon.portrait-v1`  
**Canonical filename:** `om-home-world-04-late-afternoon-portrait.png`  
**Primary reference viewport:** 390×844  
**Purpose:** Prove portrait composition geometry before producing the remaining five Home time-state portraits.

## 1. Production objective

Create or recover one portrait-authored late-afternoon Home world that is unmistakably the same physical Otaku-mori location as the approved wide late-afternoon master.

This is not a crop exercise.

The candidate must preserve the environmental narrative:

**Surface → roots → Underground**

while creating deliberate portrait framing for mobile.

## 2. Source authority

Primary wide reference:

`docs/design/references/home-world-wide-approved/om-home-world-04-late-afternoon-wide.png`

Expected current dimensions:

`1325×1187`

The wide source is visual authority for:

- sakura tree identity;
- coastline/horizon;
- castle relationship;
- roots;
- underground environment;
- warm copper/bronze late-afternoon lighting.

Before production, search for:

- larger original;
- uncropped source;
- layered source;
- portrait/mobile export;
- alternate vertical composition;
- previous owner candidate.

Any discovered material enters as `CANDIDATE`.

## 3. Composition target

Reference composition:

`390×844`

Recommended source production resolution:

- minimum `1170×2532`;
- preferred `1560×3376` if genuine source quality supports it.

Do not upscale weak art just to meet pixel targets.

## 4. Structural anchors

### Sakura tree

The tree remains the strongest natural framing element.

Requirements:
- trunk retains identity from the wide master;
- trunk may occupy roughly the left 30–45% of the upper world depending final balance;
- canopy frames rather than blankets the nav/hero areas;
- major branch direction remains plausible;
- no duplicated or malformed branch structures.

### Horizon / castle

The distant world must remain legible.

Requirements:
- preserve the sense of shoreline and distance;
- castle/landmark remains readable where present in the wide master;
- avoid reducing the world to “tree plus background blur.”

### Hero region

Approximate art-safe region:
`34–56%` of portrait height.

Requirements:
- calmer value structure;
- no dominant branch intersection behind primary heading/search;
- UI should feel embedded without covering the strongest scenic focal point.

### Root transition

Approximate region:
`52–70%`.

Requirements:
- unmistakable transition from Surface to Underground;
- roots lead the eye downward;
- no abrupt cut that suggests two separate images.

### Underground / footer

Approximate region:
`68–100%`.

Requirements:
- visible depth;
- quiet local value zones for footer groupings;
- plausible Soapstone location;
- no dependency on a full-width dark footer slab.

## 5. Late-afternoon lighting authority

Target:
- warm copper/bronze atmosphere;
- sunset-adjacent but not oversaturated orange;
- sakura remains visually distinct from bronze;
- readable tree/castle silhouettes;
- water catches believable warm light;
- Underground remains connected to the same world.

Avoid:
- orange wash over daylight;
- artificial bloom;
- neon pink;
- crushed cavern blacks;
- global vignette used to hide composition problems.

## 6. UI exclusion

Do not bake into the art:

- logo;
- navigation;
- Hero heading;
- search;
- buttons;
- Petal Wallet;
- interactive petals;
- Soapstone message;
- footer links;
- notifications.

The artwork must remain a clean environmental master.

## 7. Production method hierarchy

Use the first viable method:

1. existing owner portrait source;
2. existing portrait candidate + cleanup;
3. recomposition from larger/layered approved source;
4. controlled extension/inpainting of approved source;
5. new generation/repaint only if no usable source exists.

If generative assistance is used:
- keep approved wide art as direct reference;
- preserve world geometry;
- treat output as `CANDIDATE`;
- retain provenance and editable/source files where practical.

## 9. Pass conditions

PASS only if:
- portrait feels intentionally authored;
- same location is immediately recognizable;
- tree/horizon/castle hierarchy survives;
- Hero controls have usable quiet space;
- roots clearly descend into Underground;
- footer region works without a broad overlay;
- nearby mobile aspect ratios remain viable;
- no obvious generative/artifact damage.

## 10. Immediate rejection

Reject if:
- simple center crop;
- tree identity changes;
- castle/horizon disappear accidentally;
- malformed or repeated roots;
- Underground loses continuity;
- Hero must obscure the focal point;
- footer requires a full dark slab;
- baked UI/text is present;
- quality is lower than the wide master.
